## Script 1 — Scroll-stopper (LEAD WITH THIS)
- Hook text (0–2s): "I told an AI I didn't want to be here anymore."
- Type into David: "I feel like giving up."
- David (voice): "Hey. I'm here. I know this feels heavy. You're not alone in
  this — and God is closer than you think." + verse he pulls up.
- End card: "Meet David. Free at MyBibleAiCompanion.com"
- Caption: Sometimes you just need someone to talk to at 2am. 🤍
  #faith #christiantiktok #bibleverse

## Script 2 — Feature demo (mood → scripture)
- Hook: "POV: you don't know what to read in the Bible today."
- Open app, tap mood "Anxious" → app pulls a verse, David reads it warmly.
- David: "Take a breath. You don't have to have it all figured out right now.
  Here's something for today…" (reads verse)
- End card: "Search the Bible by how you feel. Free → MyBibleAiCompanion.com"
- Caption: Bible by mood is kind of genius ngl 📖
  #biblestudy #christian #faithjourney

## Script 3 — Relatable / shareable
- Hook: "Things I say to David that I can't say out loud:"
- Fast cuts, David responds w/ voice + verse each time:
  - "I'm so tired."
  - "I feel far from God."
  - "I don't know how to pray."
- End card: "He always has a word for you. MyBibleAiCompanion.com"
- Caption: no judgment, just Jesus 🤍 #christiantiktok #prayer #faith
